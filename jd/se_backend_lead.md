# Senior Backend Engineer – Core Services

**Company:** Meridian Systems  
**Location:** Remote (North America)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** Software Engineer

## Overview
Meridian Systems is scaling its cloud-native logistics platform and seeking a Senior Backend Engineer to lead the evolution of our core services. You will architect resilient APIs, champion observability, and collaborate closely with product and data teams to unlock new features for global shipping partners.

## Responsibilities
- Design and implement distributed services in Go and Python that power shipment orchestration.  
- Own the roadmap for API performance, including latency reduction and caching strategy.  
- Establish infrastructure-as-code templates (Terraform) and CI/CD pipelines for new microservices.  
- Partner with SRE to define observability SLIs/SLOs and incident response runbooks.  
- Mentor engineers through code reviews, pair programming, and architecture sessions.

## Requirements
- 6+ years of backend development experience with modern microservice architectures.  
- Deep knowledge of Go or Python, PostgreSQL, and event-driven systems (Kafka preferred).  
- Proven success deploying workloads to AWS or GCP using Terraform.  
- Experience with container orchestration (Kubernetes) and service mesh patterns.  
- Excellent communication skills for working across engineering and product stakeholders.

## Benefits
- Salary range: $155,000 – $185,000 base plus equity.  
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage.  
- 401(k) with company match, annual learning stipend, and flexible PTO.
