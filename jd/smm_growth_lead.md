## Job summary
HomeHype connects creators with sustainable home goods brands. We are looking for a Social Media Growth Lead to scale our presence across Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest, driving community engagement and marketplace conversions.

## Job descriptions
- Build and execute platform-specific content strategies focused on creator partnerships and product storytelling.
- Manage an editorial calendar, creative briefs, and production sprints with in-house and freelance talent.
- Launch data-driven experiments to optimize growth loops, paid amplification, and influencer collaborations.
- Analyze analytics dashboards, develop weekly insights, and communicate ROI to leadership.
- Establish social listening workflows to capture voice-of-customer insights for product and CX teams.

## Job Requirement
- 5+ years leading social media strategy for consumer brands or marketplaces.
- Track record of scaling TikTok and Instagram channels with quantifiable follower and conversion growth.
- Proficiency with Sprout Social, Later, Looker, and paid social campaign management.
- Strong copywriting skills and ability to adapt tone to emerging platform trends.
- Experience managing budgets, agency relationships, and creator contracts.

## Education
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Digital Media, Business, or related field.
- Relevant professional experience in social media growth and strategy may substitute for formal degree.
