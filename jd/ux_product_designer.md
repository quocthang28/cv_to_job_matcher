**Job title** Senior Product Designer – SaaS Collaboration
**Division:** OrbitWorks
**Location:** Remote (U.S. time zones)
**Recruiter** Full name

## Job summary
OrbitWorks helps distributed teams streamline knowledge sharing. We are searching for a Senior Product Designer to own end-to-end experience design for our core workspace product, from research synthesis to polished UI delivery.

## Job descriptions
- Conduct generative and evaluative research, translating insights into journey maps and service blueprints.
- Define information architecture, interaction patterns, and component specs using Figma and FigJam.
- Partner with PM and engineering to scope MVPs, prototype flows, and drive usability benchmarks.
- Maintain our design system, ensuring accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.2 AA) and design QA.
- Facilitate design critiques, share frameworks, and mentor teammates across the organization.

## Job Requirement
- 6+ years crafting digital B2B SaaS experiences with measurable business impact.
- Expert-level proficiency in Figma, component libraries, and collaborative design workflows.
- Proven ability to plan and execute mixed-method research, including user interviews and usability testing.
- Understanding of responsive web design, complex data visualization, and handoff to engineering.
- Excellent storytelling skills and comfort presenting to executive stakeholders.

## Education
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Design, HCI, Human Factors, Psychology, or related field.
- Equivalent professional portfolio demonstrating product design expertise may substitute for formal degree.

## Skills
- **Design Tools:** Figma (expert), FigJam, Adobe Creative Suite, prototyping tools
- **Research Methods:** User interviews, usability testing, journey mapping, service blueprints, generative research
- **Information Architecture:** IA frameworks, navigation design, content strategy
- **Design Systems:** Component libraries, design tokens, accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA)
- **Interaction Design:** User flows, wireframing, prototyping, micro-interactions
- **Data Visualization:** Complex data presentation, dashboard design, analytics interfaces
- **Collaboration:** Design QA, engineering handoff, cross-functional partnership
- **Presentation:** Stakeholder communication, design storytelling, executive presentations

## Competencies
- **End-to-End Ownership:** Takes projects from research through delivery with minimal guidance.
- **Research-Driven:** Grounds design decisions in user insights and validates through testing.
- **Systems Thinking:** Builds scalable design systems that maintain consistency across complex products.
- **Collaboration:** Partners effectively with product, engineering, and business stakeholders.
- **Accessibility Champion:** Ensures inclusive design practices and WCAG compliance.
- **Mentorship:** Shares knowledge, facilitates critiques, and elevates design practice across the organization.
