**Job title** Culinary Innovation Director – Plant-Based Brands
**Division:** Verdant Kitchen Collective
**Location:** Portland, OR (On-site)
**Recruiter** Full name

## Job summary
Verdant Kitchen Collective is a fast-growing portfolio of plant-based grab-and-go brands sold in grocery and foodservice. We are searching for a Culinary Innovation Director to lead R&D from concept through commercialization with a focus on flavor, nutrition, and scalability.

## Job descriptions
- Build the product roadmap and guide a cross-functional test kitchen team through iterative development cycles.
- Conduct sensory panels, shelf-life studies, and ingredient sourcing for national retail launches.
- Collaborate with operations to optimize co-manufacturing processes and maintain SQF Level 3 standards.
- Partner with marketing on go-to-market storytelling, packaging claims, and culinary content.
- Maintain compliance with FDA labeling, allergen, and nutritional regulations.

## Job Requirement
- 8+ years leading culinary or R&D teams within CPG, QSR, or commissary environments.
- Culinary degree or food science background with proven success scaling plant-based products.
- Expertise in product costing, commercialization timelines, and supplier negotiations.
- Strong presentation skills with the ability to translate culinary trends into business cases.
- HACCP certification and knowledge of emerging protein technologies preferred.

## Education
- Culinary degree from an accredited institution or equivalent food science degree (B.S. in Food Science, Nutrition, or related field).
- Advanced certifications in culinary arts, food safety, or plant-based nutrition are a plus.

## Skills
- **Culinary Expertise:** Advanced knife skills, recipe development, sensory evaluation, plant-based protein applications
- **Food Science:** Product formulation, shelf-life testing, texture analysis, emulsification techniques
- **Regulatory & Compliance:** FDA labeling, HACCP, SQF standards, allergen management, nutritional analysis
- **Project Management:** Product roadmap planning, cross-functional team leadership, commercialization timelines
- **Business Acumen:** Product costing, P&L analysis, supplier negotiations, margin optimization
- **Software & Tools:** PLM systems, nutritional analysis software, Microsoft Office Suite
- **Communication:** Presentation skills, culinary storytelling, go-to-market strategy

## Competencies
- **Innovation Leadership:** Ability to translate culinary trends into scalable commercial products with strong market fit.
- **Cross-functional Collaboration:** Works effectively with operations, marketing, supply chain, and quality teams.
- **Strategic Thinking:** Balances creativity with business constraints to deliver profitable product innovations.
- **Quality Focus:** Maintains rigorous standards for flavor, nutrition, and food safety throughout development.
- **Adaptability:** Thrives in fast-paced startup environment with evolving priorities and resource constraints.
- **Sustainability Mindset:** Passionate about plant-based movement and committed to environmentally conscious practices.
