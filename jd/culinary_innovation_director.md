# Culinary Innovation Director – Plant-Based Brands

**Company:** Verdant Kitchen Collective  
**Location:** Portland, OR (On-site)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** Food & Beverage Innovation

## Overview
Verdant Kitchen Collective is a fast-growing portfolio of plant-based grab-and-go brands sold in grocery and foodservice. We are searching for a Culinary Innovation Director to lead R&D from concept through commercialization with a focus on flavor, nutrition, and scalability.

## Responsibilities
- Build the product roadmap and guide a cross-functional test kitchen team through iterative development cycles.  
- Conduct sensory panels, shelf-life studies, and ingredient sourcing for national retail launches.  
- Collaborate with operations to optimize co-manufacturing processes and maintain SQF Level 3 standards.  
- Partner with marketing on go-to-market storytelling, packaging claims, and culinary content.  
- Maintain compliance with FDA labeling, allergen, and nutritional regulations.

## Requirements
- 8+ years leading culinary or R&D teams within CPG, QSR, or commissary environments.  
- Culinary degree or food science background with proven success scaling plant-based products.  
- Expertise in product costing, commercialization timelines, and supplier negotiations.  
- Strong presentation skills with the ability to translate culinary trends into business cases.  
- HACCP certification and knowledge of emerging protein technologies preferred.

## Compensation & Benefits
- Salary range: $140,000 – $170,000 plus annual profit-sharing.  
- Stock options, wellness stipend, and cafeteria plan for culinary experimentation.  
- Relocation assistance and continuing education allowances.
