# Social Content Strategist – B2B SaaS

**Company:** SyncMetrics  
**Location:** Remote (Canada & U.S.)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** Social Media Manager

## Overview
SyncMetrics delivers real-time ops analytics for SaaS finance teams. We’re hiring a Social Content Strategist to elevate our LinkedIn, X, and YouTube presence with thought leadership, product education, and customer storytelling.

## Responsibilities
- Develop integrated organic and paid content plans aligned with product launches and ABM campaigns.  
- Script, storyboard, and coordinate production for short-form video demos and customer spotlights.  
- Partner with product marketing to transform reports and webinars into social-first narratives.  
- Optimize channel performance through experimentation, SEO-informed copy, and analytics reporting.  
- Lead community management, engage with industry influencers, and nurture partner co-marketing programs.

## Requirements
- 4+ years managing B2B social channels with enterprise sales cycles.  
- Strong writing and editorial skills with a portfolio of high-performing campaign assets.  
- Familiarity with HubSpot, Salesforce, and marketing automation workflows.  
- Comfort interpreting pipeline metrics and tying social KPIs to revenue outcomes.  
- Experience coordinating with subject-matter experts and turning complex topics into compelling content.

## Benefits
- Salary range: $85,000 – $105,000 USD plus annual bonus.  
- Remote-first culture, flexible working hours, and co-working allowance.  
- Comprehensive health coverage, RRSP/401(k) match, and annual education stipend.
