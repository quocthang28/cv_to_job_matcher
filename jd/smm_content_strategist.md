**Division:** SyncMetrics
**Location:** Remote (Canada & U.S.)
**Recruiter** Full name

## Job summary
SyncMetrics delivers real-time ops analytics for SaaS finance teams. We're hiring a Social Content Strategist to elevate our LinkedIn, X, and YouTube presence with thought leadership, product education, and customer storytelling.

## Job descriptions
- Develop integrated organic and paid content plans aligned with product launches and ABM campaigns.
- Script, storyboard, and coordinate production for short-form video demos and customer spotlights.
- Partner with product marketing to transform reports and webinars into social-first narratives.
- Optimize channel performance through experimentation, SEO-informed copy, and analytics reporting.
- Lead community management, engage with industry influencers, and nurture partner co-marketing programs.

## Job Requirement
- 4+ years managing B2B social channels with enterprise sales cycles.
- Strong writing and editorial skills with a portfolio of high-performing campaign assets.
- Familiarity with HubSpot, Salesforce, and marketing automation workflows.
- Comfort interpreting pipeline metrics and tying social KPIs to revenue outcomes.
- Experience coordinating with subject-matter experts and turning complex topics into compelling content.

## Education
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, Business, or related field.
- Relevant professional experience in B2B social media strategy may substitute for formal degree.

- **Social Platforms:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X, YouTube, SlideShare
- **Content Strategy:** Editorial calendars, ABM campaigns, thought leadership positioning
- **Video Production:** Scripting, storyboarding, video editing (Premiere, Final Cut), short-form content
- **Marketing Tools:** HubSpot, Salesforce, marketing automation, CRM integration
- **Analytics:** Social media analytics, pipeline attribution, SEO, A/B testing
- **Writing & Editing:** B2B copywriting, technical translation, storytelling
- **Community Management:** Influencer engagement, partner co-marketing, industry networking

## Competencies
- **Strategic Thinking:** Aligns social strategy with product launches, sales cycles, and revenue goals.
- **Storytelling:** Transforms complex SaaS concepts into compelling narratives that resonate with finance leaders.
- **Data-Driven:** Uses analytics to optimize content performance and demonstrate ROI.
- **Collaboration:** Partners effectively with product marketing, sales, and subject-matter experts.
- **Adaptability:** Experiments with emerging formats and platforms to stay ahead of B2B trends.
- **Communication:** Excellent writing skills with ability to adjust tone for different audiences and platforms.
