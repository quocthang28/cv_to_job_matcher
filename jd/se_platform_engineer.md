## Job summary
VectorScale delivers ML-driven analytics for enterprise retailers. We are expanding our developer productivity platform team to automate build pipelines, observability, and environment provisioning for 40+ product engineers.

## Job descriptions
- Build and maintain CI/CD workflows using GitHub Actions, Argo Workflows, and Helm.
- Develop self-service tooling for ephemeral environments leveraging Kubernetes and Crossplane.
- Standardize telemetry pipelines (OpenTelemetry) and dashboards across teams.
- Harden security posture through policy-as-code, secrets management, and vulnerability scanning.
- Partner with product squads to evangelize best practices and collect platform feedback.

## Job Requirement
- 5+ years in DevOps, platform, or infrastructure engineering roles.
- Expertise with Kubernetes, Helm, and container orchestration at scale.
- Proficiency in Python or Go for automation and internal tooling.
- Experience managing observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, Tempo) and IaC (Pulumi/Terraform).
- Strong communication skills to drive adoption of platform initiatives.

## Education
