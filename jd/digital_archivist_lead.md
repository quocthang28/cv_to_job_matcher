**Job title** Digital Archivist Lead – Cultural Heritage
**Division:** Meridian Archives Consortium
**Location:** Boston, MA (Hybrid)
**Recruiter** Full name

## Job summary
Meridian Archives Consortium partners with museums and libraries to preserve endangered cultural collections. We seek a Digital Archivist Lead to architect digitization workflows and oversee long-term stewardship of multimedia assets.

## Job descriptions
- Design digitization standards spanning rare manuscripts, film, and 3D artifact scans.
- Implement metadata schemas (Dublin Core, MODS, PREMIS) and accessibility best practices.
- Manage a small team of archivists and technicians executing large-scale ingest projects.
- Evaluate storage vendors, preservation platforms, and disaster recovery strategies.
- Advocate for funding and partnerships through grant proposals and stakeholder presentations.

## Job Requirement
- MLIS or equivalent with 7+ years in archival digitization and preservation management.
- Expertise with Fedora, Archivematica, or comparable digital repository systems.
- Working knowledge of color management, media handling standards, and digital forensics.
- Track record of leading cross-institution initiatives and managing project budgets.
- Experience collaborating with Indigenous communities on culturally sensitive materials preferred.

## Education
- Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS) or Master's in Archival Studies, Digital Humanities, or related field.
- Certifications in digital preservation (e.g., DPC, SAA) or archival management are a plus.

## Skills
- **Archival Systems:** Fedora, Archivematica, DSpace, CONTENTdm, Preservica
- **Metadata Standards:** Dublin Core, MODS, PREMIS, EAD, MARC, IIIF
- **Digitization:** High-resolution scanning, color management, film digitization, 3D scanning workflows
- **Digital Preservation:** Format migration, checksums, storage architecture, disaster recovery
- **Project Management:** Budget management, grant writing, stakeholder coordination, team leadership
- **Tools & Software:** Adobe Creative Suite, DAMS platforms, digital forensics tools
- **Research & Analysis:** Archival appraisal, cultural sensitivity protocols, accessibility standards

## Competencies
- **Preservation Expertise:** Deep understanding of digital preservation theory and best practices for long-term access.
- **Leadership:** Ability to inspire and manage cross-functional teams across multiple institutions.
- **Cultural Sensitivity:** Respectful engagement with diverse communities, especially Indigenous knowledge holders.
- **Strategic Planning:** Develops comprehensive roadmaps balancing technical requirements with institutional capacity.
- **Communication:** Excellent grant writing and stakeholder presentation skills for advocacy and fundraising.
- **Adaptability:** Navigates evolving preservation technologies and emerging standards with confidence.
