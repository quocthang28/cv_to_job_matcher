**Division:** PulseWave
**Location:** Los Angeles, CA (On-site preferred)

## Job summary
PulseWave is launching a new music discovery app centered on short-form video storytelling. We're hiring a Motion & Visual Designer to craft expressive animations, micro-interactions, and visual narratives that resonate with Gen-Z audiences.

## Job descriptions
- Develop motion language guidelines, storyboards, and prototypes in After Effects and Principle.
- Collaborate with product designers to translate wireframes into immersive visuals and interactions.
- Produce marketing-ready visuals, iconography, and video assets for product launches.
- Work closely with front-end engineers to ensure animations ship with pixel-perfect fidelity.
- Conduct motion studies to measure impact on engagement and retention metrics.

## Job Requirement
- 5+ years designing motion experiences for consumer mobile or multimedia products.
- Mastery of After Effects, Cinema 4D (or similar), and advanced Figma prototyping.
- Strong visual design foundation, including typography, color theory, and illustration.
- Familiarity with Lottie, Spine, or other animation export workflows for engineering handoff.
- Portfolio demonstrating motion storytelling that elevates brand identity.

## Education
- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Motion Design, Animation, Visual Communication, or related field.
- Equivalent professional portfolio demonstrating motion design expertise may substitute for formal degree.

## Skills
- **Motion Design:** After Effects, Cinema 4D, Blender, Principle, ProtoPie
- **Design Tools:** Figma, Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop), Sketch
- **Animation Export:** Lottie, Spine, Rive, JSON-based animation workflows
- **Visual Design:** Typography, color theory, illustration, iconography, composition
- **Prototyping:** Advanced Figma prototyping, interactive motion prototypes
- **Video Production:** Editing, motion graphics, social media content formats
- **Collaboration:** Design systems, engineering handoff, developer collaboration tools

## Competencies
- **Creative Vision:** Develops distinctive motion language that strengthens brand identity and user experience.
- **Technical Proficiency:** Bridges design and engineering with production-ready animation assets.
- **User-Centric Approach:** Creates motion that enhances usability and delights users without distraction.
- **Collaboration:** Works seamlessly with product designers, engineers, and marketing teams.
- **Attention to Detail:** Ensures pixel-perfect execution and consistency across all touchpoints.
- **Trend Awareness:** Stays current with Gen-Z aesthetics and emerging motion design trends.
