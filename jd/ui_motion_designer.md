# Motion & Visual Designer – Consumer App

**Company:** PulseWave  
**Location:** Los Angeles, CA (On-site preferred)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** UI/UX Designer

## Overview
PulseWave is launching a new music discovery app centered on short-form video storytelling. We’re hiring a Motion & Visual Designer to craft expressive animations, micro-interactions, and visual narratives that resonate with Gen-Z audiences.

## Responsibilities
- Develop motion language guidelines, storyboards, and prototypes in After Effects and Principle.  
- Collaborate with product designers to translate wireframes into immersive visuals and interactions.  
- Produce marketing-ready visuals, iconography, and video assets for product launches.  
- Work closely with front-end engineers to ensure animations ship with pixel-perfect fidelity.  
- Conduct motion studies to measure impact on engagement and retention metrics.

## Requirements
- 5+ years designing motion experiences for consumer mobile or multimedia products.  
- Mastery of After Effects, Cinema 4D (or similar), and advanced Figma prototyping.  
- Strong visual design foundation, including typography, color theory, and illustration.  
- Familiarity with Lottie, Spine, or other animation export workflows for engineering handoff.  
- Portfolio demonstrating motion storytelling that elevates brand identity.

## Benefits
- Salary range: $110,000 – $135,000 plus stock options.  
- Studio environment with access to cutting-edge creative tools and production gear.  
- Comprehensive benefits, artist-in-residence program, and weekly creative workshops.
