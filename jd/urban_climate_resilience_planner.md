# Urban Climate Resilience Planner – Citywide Adaptation

**Company:** ResilientCities Group  
**Location:** Miami, FL (Hybrid)  
**Employment Type:** Contract (18 months)  
**Category:** Urban Planning

## Overview
ResilientCities Group advises coastal municipalities on climate adaptation initiatives. We are hiring an Urban Climate Resilience Planner to lead a comprehensive resilience roadmap addressing sea-level rise, heat mitigation, and equitable infrastructure investments for Miami-Dade County.

## Responsibilities
- Conduct vulnerability assessments integrating flood modeling, transportation data, and social equity metrics.  
- Facilitate community workshops, stakeholder charrettes, and public engagement campaigns.  
- Develop adaptation strategies spanning green infrastructure, zoning updates, and capital improvements.  
- Coordinate with engineering firms to prioritize project phasing and funding applications.  
- Prepare policy briefs and present progress reports to city commissioners and agency partners.

## Requirements
- Master's in urban planning, environmental science, or related field with 5+ years of applied experience.  
- Demonstrated expertise in ArcGIS Pro, InVEST, and resilience planning toolkits.  
- Strong understanding of FEMA, HUD, and state resilience grant programs.  
- Excellent facilitation skills and experience working with diverse communities.  
- AICP certification preferred; bilingual Spanish/English a plus.

## Compensation & Benefits
- Contract rate: $80 – $95 per hour plus completion bonus.  
- Health stipend, mobility allowance, and flexible work schedule.  
- Access to ResilientCities knowledge network and training resources.
