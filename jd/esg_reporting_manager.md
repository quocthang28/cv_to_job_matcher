**Job title** ESG Reporting Manager – Global Manufacturing
**Division:** TerraForge Industries
**Location:** Denver, CO (Hybrid)
**Recruiter** Full name

## Job summary
TerraForge Industries designs and manufactures precision components for the renewable energy sector. We need an ESG Reporting Manager to build a cohesive sustainability data program and ensure transparent disclosures across global operations.

## Job descriptions
- Own annual ESG and impact reporting aligned with GRI, SASB, and CSRD standards.
- Consolidate emissions, waste, safety, and supply chain data from 20+ facilities worldwide.
- Partner with finance, legal, and operations to translate ESG metrics into investor-ready narratives.
- Implement data quality controls and audit trails within the sustainability data warehouse.
- Track regulatory developments and brief executive leadership on emerging disclosure requirements.

## Job Requirement
- 6+ years in ESG, corporate sustainability, or financial reporting for industrial enterprises.
- Deep knowledge of carbon accounting frameworks and lifecycle assessment fundamentals.
- Advanced Excel and experience with Workiva, Persefoni, or comparable reporting platforms.
- Excellent stakeholder management skills across technical and executive teams.
- CPA, CFA, or ISSP-SA certification is a plus.

## Education
- Bachelor's degree in Environmental Science, Sustainability, Finance, Accounting, or related field.
- Master's degree in Sustainability, MBA, or relevant certifications (CPA, CFA, ISSP-SA) preferred.

## Skills
- **ESG Frameworks:** GRI, SASB, TCFD, CSRD, CDP, UN SDGs
- **Carbon Accounting:** Scope 1/2/3 emissions, GHG Protocol, lifecycle assessment (LCA)
- **Reporting Platforms:** Workiva, Persefoni, Enablon, Brightest
- **Data Management:** Advanced Excel, SQL, data quality controls, audit trails
- **Financial Reporting:** Understanding of investor relations, materiality assessments, integrated reporting
- **Regulatory Knowledge:** SEC climate disclosure rules, EU taxonomy, emerging ESG regulations
- **Communication:** Narrative development, executive briefings, stakeholder engagement

## Competencies
- **Analytical Rigor:** Ensures data accuracy and integrity across complex global operations.
- **Strategic Thinking:** Anticipates regulatory trends and positions organization proactively.
- **Cross-functional Leadership:** Effectively collaborates with finance, legal, operations, and executive teams.
- **Communication Excellence:** Translates technical ESG metrics into compelling narratives for investors and stakeholders.
- **Detail Orientation:** Maintains meticulous documentation and audit-ready processes.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Stays current on evolving ESG standards and incorporates best practices.
