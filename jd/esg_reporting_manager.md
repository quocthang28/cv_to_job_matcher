# ESG Reporting Manager – Global Manufacturing

**Company:** TerraForge Industries  
**Location:** Denver, CO (Hybrid)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** Sustainability & Compliance

## Overview
TerraForge Industries designs and manufactures precision components for the renewable energy sector. We need an ESG Reporting Manager to build a cohesive sustainability data program and ensure transparent disclosures across global operations.

## Responsibilities
- Own annual ESG and impact reporting aligned with GRI, SASB, and CSRD standards.  
- Consolidate emissions, waste, safety, and supply chain data from 20+ facilities worldwide.  
- Partner with finance, legal, and operations to translate ESG metrics into investor-ready narratives.  
- Implement data quality controls and audit trails within the sustainability data warehouse.  
- Track regulatory developments and brief executive leadership on emerging disclosure requirements.

## Requirements
- 6+ years in ESG, corporate sustainability, or financial reporting for industrial enterprises.  
- Deep knowledge of carbon accounting frameworks and lifecycle assessment fundamentals.  
- Advanced Excel and experience with Workiva, Persefoni, or comparable reporting platforms.  
- Excellent stakeholder management skills across technical and executive teams.  
- CPA, CFA, or ISSP-SA certification is a plus.

## Benefits
- Salary range: $125,000 – $150,000 with annual performance bonus.  
- 401(k) with 5% match, comprehensive health coverage, and paid parental leave.  
- Budget for professional certifications and sustainability conferences.
