**Job title** UX Researcher – Fintech Platform
**Division:** LedgerLeap
**Location:** San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)
**Recruiter** Full name

## Job summary
LedgerLeap simplifies treasury workflows for high-growth startups. We need a UX Researcher to uncover customer needs across finance teams and transform insights into actionable product direction.

## Job descriptions
- Plan and execute research studies including contextual inquiry, diary studies, and usability testing.
- Build research roadmaps aligned with product strategy and quantify opportunity areas.
- Analyze qualitative and quantitative data using Dovetail, Looker, and survey tools.
- Deliver clear research readouts, personas, and journey frameworks that inform roadmap decisions.
- Coach product and design partners on best practices for evidence-based decision making.

## Job Requirement
- 4+ years conducting applied UX research within SaaS or fintech products.
- Skilled in mixed methods, recruitment planning, and experiment design.
- Experience crafting compelling narratives for executive stakeholders.
- Familiarity with accessibility and compliance considerations in financial applications.
- Ability to collaborate closely with designers, PMs, and engineers in a fast-paced environment.

## Education
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in HCI, Psychology, Anthropology, Cognitive Science, or related field.
- Ph.D. in relevant field or equivalent professional research experience is a plus.

## Skills
- **Research Methods:** Contextual inquiry, diary studies, usability testing, interviews, surveys, card sorting
- **Mixed Methods:** Qualitative and quantitative research design, triangulation, experiment design
- **Research Tools:** Dovetail, UserTesting, Qualtrics, Lookback, Optimal Workshop
- **Analytics:** Looker, Mixpanel, Google Analytics, statistical analysis
- **Synthesis:** Affinity mapping, thematic analysis, persona development, journey mapping
- **Communication:** Research readouts, storytelling, executive presentations
- **Specialized Knowledge:** Fintech workflows, compliance considerations, accessibility research

## Competencies
- **Research Excellence:** Expert in diverse qualitative and quantitative methodologies.
- **Strategic Impact:** Aligns research with product strategy and identifies high-value opportunity areas.
- **Storytelling:** Transforms complex data into compelling narratives that drive decision-making.
- **Collaboration:** Partners closely with product, design, and engineering in agile environments.
- **Empathy:** Deep understanding of user needs, especially in complex financial workflows.
- **Advocacy:** Champions user-centered design and coaches teams on research best practices.
