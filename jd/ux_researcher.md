# UX Researcher – Fintech Platform

**Company:** LedgerLeap  
**Location:** San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** UI/UX Designer

## Overview
LedgerLeap simplifies treasury workflows for high-growth startups. We need a UX Researcher to uncover customer needs across finance teams and transform insights into actionable product direction.

## Responsibilities
- Plan and execute research studies including contextual inquiry, diary studies, and usability testing.  
- Build research roadmaps aligned with product strategy and quantify opportunity areas.  
- Analyze qualitative and quantitative data using Dovetail, Looker, and survey tools.  
- Deliver clear research readouts, personas, and journey frameworks that inform roadmap decisions.  
- Coach product and design partners on best practices for evidence-based decision making.

## Requirements
- 4+ years conducting applied UX research within SaaS or fintech products.  
- Skilled in mixed methods, recruitment planning, and experiment design.  
- Experience crafting compelling narratives for executive stakeholders.  
- Familiarity with accessibility and compliance considerations in financial applications.  
- Ability to collaborate closely with designers, PMs, and engineers in a fast-paced environment.

## Compensation & Benefits
- Salary range: $120,000 – $145,000 plus annual bonus.  
- Equity grants, commuter stipend, and wellness reimbursement.  
- Comprehensive insurance, mental health support, and 16 weeks parental leave.
