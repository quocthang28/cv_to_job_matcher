**Job title** Community & Engagement Manager – Gaming Brand
**Division:** NexusArcade
**Location:** Los Angeles, CA (Hybrid)
**Recruiter** Full name

## Job summary
NexusArcade builds cross-platform gaming experiences and live events. We are searching for a Community & Engagement Manager to cultivate vibrant online communities across Discord, Twitch, and emerging platforms while supporting global event activations.

## Job descriptions
- Design community programming including AMAs, tournaments, and creator collaborations.
- Moderate Discord servers, implement community guidelines, and respond to member feedback.
- Coordinate live-stream schedules, overlays, and chat engagement strategies with our broadcast team.
- Track sentiment, retention, and engagement metrics to inform product and marketing roadmaps.
- Partner with support to manage escalation workflows and maintain a positive player experience.

## Job Requirement
- 3+ years managing gaming or entertainment communities across Discord, Twitch, and Twitter/X.
- Strong understanding of moderation tooling, bots, and community analytics platforms.
- Excellent interpersonal skills with experience handling crisis communications.
- Ability to travel 10% for live events, conventions, and community meetups.
- Passion for gaming culture and emerging interactive formats.

## Education
- Bachelor's degree in Communications, Marketing, Public Relations, Game Studies, or related field.
- Relevant professional experience in community management may substitute for formal degree.

## Skills
- **Community Platforms:** Discord, Twitch, Reddit, Twitter/X, YouTube, TikTok
- **Moderation Tools:** Discord bots (MEE6, Dyno), AutoMod, community safety systems
- **Analytics:** Community metrics, sentiment analysis, engagement tracking tools
- **Content Creation:** Basic video editing, graphic design, live-stream production knowledge
- **Event Coordination:** Tournament organization, live event logistics, creator partnerships
- **Communication:** Crisis management, conflict resolution, customer service excellence
- **Gaming Knowledge:** Deep understanding of gaming culture, trends, and player psychology

## Competencies
- **Community Building:** Creates inclusive, engaged communities that foster loyalty and positive experiences.
- **Empathy:** Understands player perspectives and advocates for community needs internally.
- **Crisis Management:** Handles sensitive situations with tact and maintains community trust during challenges.
- **Creativity:** Designs innovative programming that drives engagement and retention.
- **Adaptability:** Thrives in fast-paced gaming environment with evolving trends and platforms.
- **Collaboration:** Works cross-functionally with product, marketing, support, and broadcast teams.
