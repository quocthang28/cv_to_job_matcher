# Satellite Operations Specialist – Low Earth Orbit Fleet

**Company:** OrbitalVantage Labs  
**Location:** Austin, TX (Remote-first)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** Aerospace Operations

## Overview
OrbitalVantage Labs operates a constellation of low Earth orbit microsatellites delivering real-time Earth observation data. We are hiring a Satellite Operations Specialist to monitor spacecraft health, plan maneuvers, and support mission control activities around the clock.

## Responsibilities
- Manage daily telemetry and command operations using the MissionControl-X platform.  
- Analyze subsystem performance, detect anomalies, and coordinate rapid response procedures.  
- Plan orbit maintenance burns, collision avoidance strategies, and payload scheduling.  
- Collaborate with ground station partners to optimize pass coverage and data downlink efficiency.  
- Contribute to runbooks, training materials, and cross-team post-mission reviews.

## Requirements
- 4+ years in satellite operations, mission planning, or aerospace systems engineering.  
- Proficiency with STK, GMAT, or equivalent orbital mechanics software.  
- Familiarity with CCSDS protocols, RF communications, and secure command chains.  
- Ability to work rotating shifts and support off-hours incident response.  
- Active DoD Secret clearance or ability to obtain within 6 months.

## Compensation & Benefits
- Salary range: $95,000 – $125,000 plus night shift differential.  
- Equity grants, comprehensive health benefits, and 401(k) with company contribution.  
- Home office stipend and annual professional development budget.
