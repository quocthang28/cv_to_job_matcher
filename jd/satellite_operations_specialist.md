## Job summary
OrbitalVantage Labs operates a constellation of low Earth orbit microsatellites delivering real-time Earth observation data. We are hiring a Satellite Operations Specialist to monitor spacecraft health, plan maneuvers, and support mission control activities around the clock.

## Job descriptions
- Manage daily telemetry and command operations using the MissionControl-X platform.
- Analyze subsystem performance, detect anomalies, and coordinate rapid response procedures.
- Plan orbit maintenance burns, collision avoidance strategies, and payload scheduling.
- Collaborate with ground station partners to optimize pass coverage and data downlink efficiency.
- Contribute to runbooks, training materials, and cross-team post-mission reviews.

## Job Requirement
- 4+ years in satellite operations, mission planning, or aerospace systems engineering.
- Proficiency with STK, GMAT, or equivalent orbital mechanics software.
- Familiarity with CCSDS protocols, RF communications, and secure command chains.
- Ability to work rotating shifts and support off-hours incident response.
- Active DoD Secret clearance or ability to obtain within 6 months.

## Education
- Bachelor's degree in Aerospace Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Physics, or related technical field.
- Master's degree in aerospace or related discipline is a plus.

## Skills
- **Orbital Mechanics:** STK (Systems Tool Kit), GMAT, orbit determination, trajectory planning
- **Satellite Operations:** Telemetry analysis, commanding, anomaly resolution, mission control systems
- **Communication Protocols:** CCSDS, RF link analysis, ground station coordination
- **Software & Tools:** MissionControl-X platform, Python/MATLAB for analysis, scripting
- **Systems Engineering:** Spacecraft subsystems (ADCS, power, thermal, communications)
- **Security:** Secure command protocols, clearance handling, ITAR compliance
- **Documentation:** Runbook development, technical writing, knowledge transfer

## Competencies
- **Technical Expertise:** Deep understanding of spacecraft operations and orbital mechanics.
- **Problem-Solving:** Rapid anomaly detection and resolution under time-critical conditions.
- **Attention to Detail:** Meticulous monitoring of telemetry and system health indicators.
- **Collaboration:** Effective coordination with ground stations, engineering teams, and mission leadership.
- **Adaptability:** Comfortable with rotating shifts and off-hours incident response.
- **Communication:** Clear technical documentation and concise status reporting during high-stakes operations.
