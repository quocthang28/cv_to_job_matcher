# Machine Learning Engineer – Recommendation Systems

**Company:** AuroraStream  
**Location:** Seattle, WA (Hybrid)  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Category:** Software Engineer

## Overview
AuroraStream personalizes streaming media experiences for 50M+ monthly users. We are hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to advance our recommendation engines and experimentation platform.

## Responsibilities
- Design, train, and deploy ranking and retrieval models using PyTorch and Spark.  
- Build feature pipelines and data quality checks with Feature Store integrations.  
- Instrument online experiments, analyze results, and iterate quickly on model strategies.  
- Collaborate with data scientists and product managers to translate hypotheses into deployable solutions.  
- Maintain model observability, including drift detection and automated rollback mechanisms.

## Requirements
- 4+ years building production ML systems with large-scale datasets.  
- Proficiency in Python, PyTorch/TensorFlow, and distributed processing frameworks.  
- Experience deploying models to Kubernetes or SageMaker with CI/CD automation.  
- Familiarity with feature stores, MLflow, and experimentation platforms.  
- Strong statistical reasoning and ability to communicate insights to non-technical partners.

## Compensation & Benefits
- Salary range: $160,000 – $190,000 plus RSUs and annual bonus.  
- Comprehensive health coverage, fertility benefits, and 18 weeks parental leave.  
- On-site wellness center, transit pass, and generous continuing-education budget.
